Seasonal crops are cultivated based on climatic conditions and rainfall patterns.

Kharif Crops:
Sown during monsoon (June to July) and harvested in October to November.
Examples include rice, maize, cotton, groundnut, and soybean.
Require warm temperature and high rainfall.

Rabi Crops:
Sown in winter (October to November) and harvested in March to April.
Examples include wheat, barley, mustard, and gram.
Require cool climate and low rainfall.

Zaid Crops:
Grown between Rabi and Kharif seasons (March to June).
Examples include watermelon, cucumber, and fodder crops.
Require irrigation support.

Crop Selection Factors:
Soil type, water availability, temperature, and market demand.
Use of seasonal calendars improves yield and reduces risk.

Climate Change Impact:
Irregular rainfall and temperature fluctuations affect seasonal crop planning.
Adoption of climate-resilient varieties is recommended.
