Terrace gardening is the practice of growing plants on rooftops using containers or raised beds.

Advantages of Terrace Gardening:
Efficient use of unused rooftop space.
Improves home insulation and reduces heat.
Provides fresh vegetables and fruits.
Enhances urban biodiversity.

Structural Safety:
Ensure the terrace can support additional load.
Use lightweight containers and potting mix.
Ensure proper waterproofing and drainage.

Suitable Crops:
Vegetables like tomato, brinjal, chili, spinach, beans, and cucumber.
Fruits such as papaya, lemon, guava, and strawberry.
Ornamental plants and flowering plants.

Growing Media:
Use a mix of garden soil, cocopeat, compost, and sand.
Avoid heavy clay soil.
Maintain good aeration and drainage.

Water Management:
Drip irrigation is ideal for terrace gardens.
Avoid water stagnation to prevent roof damage.
Use trays to collect excess water.

Sunlight Requirement:
Most vegetables require 6 to 8 hours of sunlight.
Leafy greens can tolerate partial shade.

Common Problems:
Heat stress during summer.
Wind damage to tall plants.
Nutrient deficiency due to limited soil volume.

Preventive Measures:
Provide shade nets in summer.
Use organic fertilizers regularly.
Support plants using stakes or trellis.
