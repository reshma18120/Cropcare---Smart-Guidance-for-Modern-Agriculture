Kitchen gardening is the practice of growing vegetables, herbs, and fruits near the home for household consumption.

Benefits of Kitchen Gardening:
Provides fresh, chemical-free vegetables.
Reduces household food expenses.
Improves nutrition and food security.
Encourages recycling of kitchen waste through composting.

Suitable Crops:
Leafy vegetables like spinach, amaranthus, coriander, and fenugreek.
Vegetables such as tomato, brinjal, chili, okra, beans, carrot, and radish.
Herbs like mint, basil, curry leaves, and tulsi.

Site Selection:
Choose a location with at least 5 to 6 hours of sunlight.
Ensure proper drainage and access to water.
Containers, grow bags, and raised beds can be used.

Soil Preparation:
Use fertile soil mixed with compost or vermicompost.
Soil should be loose, well-drained, and rich in organic matter.
Maintain soil pH between 6.0 and 7.5.

Watering:
Water regularly but avoid waterlogging.
Early morning or evening watering is recommended.
Mulching helps retain moisture.

Pest and Disease Control:
Use neem oil spray for aphids and mites.
Remove infected plants immediately.
Encourage natural pest predators.

Harvesting:
Harvest leafy vegetables regularly to encourage new growth.
Pick vegetables at proper maturity for best taste and yield.
